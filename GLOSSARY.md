# Golf Practice

A personal record of on-course performance that turns strokes gained into targeted practice at the range.

## Language

### On the course

**Round**:
A single played round of golf, recorded with its date, course details, score, Handicap Index, Course Handicap and Strokes Gained per SG Category.
_Avoid_: Game (reserved for practice), scorecard

**Handicap Index**:
The golfer's portable measure of playing ability on the day of a Round, independent of any course.
_Avoid_: Handicap (on its own), Proficiency, Skill

**Course Handicap**:
The number of strokes the golfer receives on the course and tees of a particular Round.
_Avoid_: Handicap (on its own), playing handicap

**Strokes Gained**:
A number expressing how many strokes better or worse a golfer performed against a baseline, entered as an already-calculated value.
_Avoid_: SG score, rating

**SG Category**:
One of the four parts of the game that Strokes Gained is split into: Off the Tee, Approach, Around the Green, Putting.
_Avoid_: Area, skill area

**Baseline**:
The standard of golfer that Strokes Gained is measured against, such as Tour, scratch or a handicap level.
_Avoid_: Benchmark, par

### In practice

**Practice Session**:
An ordered plan of Drills and Games for one visit, fitted to the golfer's available balls or time and Facilities.
_Avoid_: Workout, plan, routine

**Weekly Plan**:
The set of Practice Sessions for a week, generated from recent Rounds and the time the golfer has available that week.
_Avoid_: Schedule, program

**Unplanned Session**:
A Practice Session generated on the spot, outside any Weekly Plan.
_Avoid_: Ad-hoc session, quick session

**Drill**:
Repetitive practice aimed at technique or feel, with no score.
_Avoid_: Exercise

**Game**:
A defined practice challenge with rules and a numeric score that can be compared over time.
_Avoid_: Challenge, test, Round

**Variation**:
A version of a Game that changes only its setup details or scenario, leaving its rules, scoring and difficulty unchanged.
_Avoid_: Remix, new Game

**Facility**:
A type of practice area that a Drill or Game needs: range bay, chipping area, putting green or practice bunker.
_Avoid_: Venue, location

**Practice Ground**:
A named place where the golfer practises, defined by which Facilities it has and the features of each.
_Avoid_: Venue, club, range

### Measuring the golfer

**Skill**:
How the golfer performs on the course in an SG Category, derived from Rounds. It determines what to practise.
_Avoid_: Ability, level

**Proficiency**:
How well the golfer performs at a specific Game, derived only from that Game's scores and expressed as a Level. It determines how hard the Game should be.
_Avoid_: Skill, handicap, rating

**Level**:
A numbered step of difficulty for a Game, each defined by that Game's own difficulty settings and target scores.
_Avoid_: Tier, grade, difficulty score
